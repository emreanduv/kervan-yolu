Wind turbine logistics simulator: drive a truck from the port to a wind farm in the hills hauling tower sections, blades, SCADA panels and spare parts. Four regions (Marmara, the Aegean, Central Anatolia and Thrace), a blade lifter, snow chains, crosswinds, 1923 jobs, forklift loading, drone damage inspection and realistic freight pay.

JOBS
• Crate job: load the SCADA panel, switchgear and spare part crates onto the trailer with a forklift and strap them down.
• Tower section: a heavy load on a lowbed trailer; manage gears and brakes on the climbs.
• Turbine blade: a 72 m blade on an extendable trailer, root frame up front, tip frame at the back, self-steering rear bogie. Take bends wide and keep off the banks.
• Nacelle: a 90 ton machine house, the heaviest load; slow on the climbs and brake before the bends.
• Hub, drive train (main shaft and gearbox), anchor cage and material container: each with its own weight, width and wind area.
• Urgent spare part: a turbine is down; short deadline, high pay.
• Return job: take the empty blade racks back to the port.

PORT, CUSTOMS AND PERMITS
• Imported cargo comes off the ship by port crane and goes through customs inspection in the bonded area.
• Heavy and oversize loads need an abnormal load road permit, a road use fee and, where needed, a road modification protocol; papers are checked at the port exit gate.

TRAFFIC RULES
• Tachograph: a 45 min break at the rest area after 4.5 hours; blades wait at port in winds over 15 m/s.
• Trucks: 80 km/h on open roads, 50 in villages, 30 at the port and on site; special-permit heavy and wide loads max 60, with chains 40. The limit is on the sign next to the speed.
• Speed cameras: speeding adds a fine to the job report.
• Keep right: oncoming cars, minibuses, coaches and tractors. Wide loads get escort vehicles front and rear; oncoming traffic pulls over.
• Loads carry the turbine maker's label; the site sign shows the investor and wind farm.

DELIVERY AND DAMAGE
• Fly the drone at delivery and scan the cargo: scratches, dents and coating damage go into the report.
• Going off road, taking bends too fast, hard braking and collisions damage the cargo; the warning tells you which speed to slow down to.
• Job report: freight fee, damage deduction, insurance payout (after the deductible), late penalty and damage-free bonus.

REGIONS
• Marmara: from Bandırma Port to the Balıkesir Uplands. Container crane, cargo ship, a coastal road and a caravanserai by the roadside.
• Aegean: from Aliağa Port to the Bergama Hills. Mountain roads, olive groves, red pines and narrow village streets lined with cypresses.
• Central Anatolia: from Samsun Port to the snowy Sivas Plateau. Icy roads above the snow line, blizzards, snow-covered pines and Kangal dogs guarding a sheepfold by the road.
• Thrace: from Tekirdağ Port to the turbine ridges of Mount Ganos. Sunflower and wheat fields, rows of poplars, windsocks and gusting crosswinds.
Morning, noon, evening and night drives; at night, drive by your headlights.

BLADE LIFTER
On the Aegean route the 72 metre blade cannot enter the narrow village street on the long trailer. At the transfer yard a mobile crane moves it to the blade lifter; in the village tilt the blade up and lower it before the power line. Crane and lifter hire are costs, the lifter surcharge is income.

SNOW CHAINS
On the icy climbs to Sivas the truck spins and the brakes weaken. Stop at the chain-up area, fit the chains and climb at up to 40 km/h.

CROSSWINDS
In Thrace, watch the wind gauge: as the wind gusts, a blade or tower pulls like a sail and the truck drifts. The game tells you which speed to slow down to.

THE CAB
Live gauges, steering wheel, tachograph, prayer beads hanging from the windscreen and a tulip tea glass on the dashboard.
