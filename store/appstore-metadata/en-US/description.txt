Wind turbine logistics simulator: haul tower sections, blades, nacelles and spare parts by truck from the port to a hilltop wind farm. Eight regions, a blade lifter, snow chains, crosswinds, rain and mud, your own haulage company, a 10 turbine wind farm project and 1923 jobs.

JOBS
• Turbine blade: a 72 m blade on an extendable trailer with a self-steering rear bogie. Take bends wide.
• Tower section and 90 ton nacelle: manage gears and brakes on the climbs, slow down before the bends.
• Hub, drive train, anchor cage, container: each with its own weight, width and wind area.
• Crate job: load the SCADA panel and spare parts with a forklift and strap them down.
• Urgent spare parts and empty rack returns.

PORT, PAPERS AND PERMITS
• Ship discharge by port crane, customs inspection, and a paper check at the gate for every load leaving the port.
• Abnormal load permit, road use fee, road modification protocol.
• Blades and towers move only at night in Marmara and Thrace; police or gendarmerie lead blade and nacelle convoys.
• Survey the route by drone before you leave: bends, villages, power lines, weigh station and fuel are marked on the map.

ROAD RULES
• Tachograph: a 45 min break at the rest area every 4.5 hours. Refuel at the fuel station; heavy loads must cross the weigh station.
• Trucks: 80 km/h on open roads, 50 in villages; abnormal loads 30 in built-up areas, 50 outside, 40 with chains. Speed cameras issue fines.
• Keep right: oncoming sedans, SUVs, pickups, minibuses, coaches, trucks and tractors light their brake lamps and put on hazards when they yield. Escorts front and rear; the radio calls out real traffic.

DELIVERY AND DAMAGE
• Scan the cargo by drone at delivery: scratches, dents and coating damage go into the report.
• Fast bends, hard braking, going off road and collisions damage the cargo; warnings tell you which speed to slow down to.
• Job report: freight, itemised costs, damage, insurance, late penalty and damage-free bonus.

REGIONS
• Marmara: Bandırma Port to the Balıkesir Uplands; coastal road and caravanserai.
• Aegean: Aliağa to the Bergama Hills; blade lifter in a narrow village street and a power line.
• Central Anatolia: Samsun to the snowy Sivas Plateau; icy road, snow chains and Kangal dogs.
• Thrace: Tekirdağ to Mount Ganos; sunflower fields and gusting crosswinds.
• Çanakkale: Karabiga Port to the Biga Hills through olive groves.
• Hatay: İskenderun to the Belen Pass; citrus orchards and strong winds.
• Black Sea: Trabzon to the Çaykara highland; tea gardens, granaries, fog and snow.
• Cappadocia: Mersin to the fairy chimneys and the foothills of Mount Erciyes.
Morning, noon, evening and night; roads get slippery in the rain, and a dozer tows you out of the mud on site. Village elders wave from their tea table.

COMPANY AND PROJECT
Build your own haulage company: buy a telescopic trailer, blade lifter, SPMT and new trucks, take loans, hire drivers, unlock skills. In the 10 turbine wind farm project, bring every part on time so the crane never waits.
