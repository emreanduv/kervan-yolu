Wind turbine logistics simulator: drive a truck from the port to a wind farm in the hills hauling tower sections, blades, SCADA panels and spare parts. Three regions (Marmara, the Aegean and Central Anatolia), a blade lifter, snow chains, 1923 jobs, forklift loading, drone damage inspection and realistic freight pay.

JOBS
• Crate job: load the SCADA panel, switchgear and spare part crates onto the trailer with a forklift and strap them down.
• Tower section: a heavy load on a lowbed trailer; manage gears and brakes on the climbs.
• Turbine blade: a 40 metre blade sticking out 30 metres behind the trailer. Take corners wide and keep it off the slopes.
• Urgent spare part: a turbine is down; short deadline, high pay.
• Return job: take the empty blade racks back to the port.

DELIVERY AND DAMAGE
• Fly the drone at delivery and scan the cargo: scratches, dents and coating damage go into the report.
• Going off road, taking bends too fast, hard braking and collisions damage the cargo; the warning tells you which speed to slow down to.
• Job report: freight fee, damage deduction, insurance payout (after the deductible), late penalty and damage-free bonus.

REGIONS
• Marmara: from Bandırma Port to the Balıkesir Uplands. Container crane, cargo ship, a coastal road and a caravanserai by the roadside.
• Aegean: from Aliağa Port to the Bergama Hills. Mountain roads, olive groves, red pines and narrow village streets lined with cypresses.
• Central Anatolia: from Samsun Port to the snowy Sivas Plateau. Icy roads above the snow line, blizzards, snow-covered pines and Kangal dogs guarding a sheepfold by the road.
Morning, noon, evening and night drives; at night, drive by your headlights.

BLADE LIFTER
In the narrow Aegean village street a 40 metre blade hits the houses. Stop, tilt the blade up from its root and carry it over the roofs. A power line follows the village: stop in front of it and lower the blade.

SNOW CHAINS
On the icy climbs to Sivas the truck spins and the brakes weaken. Stop at the chain-up area, fit the chains and climb at up to 40 km/h.

THE CAB
Live gauges, steering wheel, tachograph, prayer beads hanging from the windscreen and a tulip tea glass on the dashboard.

GAMEPLAY
• Free Drive: no jobs and no clock; pick the time of day and enjoy the road.
• The first 3 jobs are free. The Full Version (one-time purchase) unlocks all 1923 jobs.
• Turkish and English, no ads, works offline, collects no data.
