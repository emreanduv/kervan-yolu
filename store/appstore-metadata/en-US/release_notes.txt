1.1 Site update:
- Route survey and dummy run: branches, signs, power lines, tight bends and other obstacles are reported before the haul and you choose which to fix.
- Crane work after every delivery: tower, nacelle, hub and blades are assembled on the turbine, the anchor cage goes into the foundation pit, other loads are craned onto the laydown area.
- Roadside lay-bys: fuel, weigh station and chain-up are done in the paved lay-by on the right, announced by a blue P sign.
- 80 m blades, low tip frame, level crossings with trains, reverse manoeuvre at tight sites.
- Seasonal nature: autumn trees turn yellow, patchy snow on the ground.
- Smoother mountain asphalt, the forklift no longer drives through the truck, fairer road work costs.
