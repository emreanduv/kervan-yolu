## App Store inceleme notu (App Review Information → Notes)
Kervan Yolu is a single-player, offline truck driving simulator about wind turbine logistics. No account or login is needed; no ads, no data collection. On launch the game asks for the language (Turkish/English). The first tab is Free Drive (no jobs). The second tab, Jobs, starts job 1: load crates with the forklift (drive the forklift to the crate marked by the yellow arrow, tap LIFT, drive to the trailer, tap DROP), strap the cargo, get in the truck and drive to the green ring at the wind farm, then tap the markers on the cargo to complete the drone inspection. Jobs 1–3 are free; the one-time Full Version in-app purchase (non-consumable) unlocks jobs 4–1923 and can be restored from Settings ("Restore purchase"). Settings (gear icon) contains How to Play, Rate, Feedback (opens mail), Recommend and Delete my data.
